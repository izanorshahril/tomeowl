# Tomeowl evidence workspace

Tomeowl connects local sources through inspectable evidence and presents those sources as a navigable workspace.
The map conveys the organization and recorded relationships of evidence without turning visual proximity into a claim.

## Language

**Corpus**:
A declared set of sources collected for a particular task, such as project discovery or a research review.
_Avoid_: Whole machine, knowledge universe

**Collection**:
A named presentation group of sources within a corpus.
Membership indicates organization, not a factual relationship between members.
_Avoid_: Topic certainty, dependency

**Source**:
An original document, transcript, or other evidence-bearing item with a stable identity.
_Avoid_: Memory, fact

**Revision**:
A particular observed content state of a source.
Evidence refers to the revision from which it was obtained.
_Avoid_: Latest truth

**Evidence**:
A located passage or record from a named source revision that can support an inspection or claim.
_Avoid_: Summary, interpretation

**Locator**:
An address into a source revision, such as a line interval or transcript time interval.
_Avoid_: Approximate pointer

**Recorded relationship**:
A typed link between sources whose method and supporting evidence are available for inspection.
Its existence does not imply that every interpretation of the link is verified.
_Avoid_: Relatedness, spatial closeness

**Candidate relationship**:
A suggested link that has not completed the applicable review or acceptance process.
_Avoid_: Fact, confirmed dependency

**Similarity**:
A measured resemblance under a named method and reference point.
It is a discovery signal rather than proof of a relationship.
_Avoid_: Evidence, confidence

**Overview**:
The corpus-level view of named collections, coverage, and recorded relationship counts.
_Avoid_: All-node cloud

**Neighborhood**:
A bounded view of a selected collection or source and its relevant sources or recorded relationships.
_Avoid_: Semantic neighborhood when membership alone is shown

**View profile**:
A saved choice of presentation preferences, filters, and camera state that does not change source evidence.
_Avoid_: Knowledge state

**Context packet**:
A bounded selection of evidence intended to answer a query or inform a subsequent task.
Native packets distinguish keyword matches from passages reached through recorded links and retain the path citations.
Their budgets count all unique cited sources/chunks and all quoted characters, including supporting link evidence.
_Avoid_: Durable memory

**Finding**:
An interpretation that cites supporting evidence and states its assumptions or review status.
_Avoid_: Source fact

**Literature review**:
A deliberate comparison of selected works, their findings, methods, and evidence, with retained screening and interpretation decisions.
A map of research notes can support this activity without completing it.
_Avoid_: Automatic scholarly synthesis
